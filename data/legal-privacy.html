Locksmith Express MK ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through www.locksmithexpressmk.com, how we use it, and your choices.

Information we collect

When you submit a form or contact us, we collect the details you provide — such as your name, phone number, email address, location (city or ZIP), the service you need and any message. We also collect basic technical data such as the page you visited, referring website and campaign tags (for example UTM parameters) to understand how people find us.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your request, give you a quote and dispatch a technician.
  • To contact you by phone, text message or email about your service request.
  • To improve our website, services and advertising.

SMS / text messaging

If you check the consent box on our forms, you agree to receive calls and text messages from Locksmith Express MK about your request. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run our business (such as our customer-management, email and phone systems), and when required by law.

Cookies & analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar tools (such as Google Analytics, Google Ads and Meta) to measure visits and the performance of our ads. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Data security & retention

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information and keep it only as long as needed to provide our services and meet legal obligations.
