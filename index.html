FAQ

Are you really open 24/7, including holidays?

Yes. Locksmith Express MK is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week — including nights, weekends and holidays. Emergency service is always open.

How fast can a locksmith get to me in Springfield, MA?

We're a mobile locksmith, so we dispatch a technician straight to your location anywhere in Springfield and Western Massachusetts. Arrival time depends on where you are and on traffic — call (413) 433-1135 and we'll give you a real ETA on the phone.

Can you make a new car key if I lost all my keys?

Yes. We make and program new car keys on-site — transponder keys, remotes, key fobs and push-to-start smart keys — so there's no need to tow your vehicle to a dealership.

Will unlocking my car or door cause damage?

No. We use professional no-damage entry methods for car lockouts and for home and business lockouts, so you get back in without harm to your doors, windows or locks.

Do you program key fobs and push-to-start keys?

Yes. We program key fobs, remotes, transponder (chip) keys and push-to-start smart keys for most makes and models — and we can make a spare so you always have a backup.

Should I rekey or replace my locks after moving?

Rekeying changes the inside of the lock so old keys stop working, without replacing the full hardware — a fast, budget-friendly choice after moving in or losing keys. If your locks are worn, damaged or outdated, we'll recommend a lock change or a high-security upgrade.

How much does a locksmith cost?

It depends on the service, your vehicle or lock type, and the time of day. Call (413) 433-1135 or send the form with your details and we'll give you a clear quote.

Can you open a safe?

Yes — safe opening, safe combination changes, and safe lock repair and replacement.
