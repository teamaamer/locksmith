    Frequently Asked Questions

    Can You Replace a Damaged Nissan or Infiniti Key Shell?

    Yes. If the internal electronics and transponder chip are still working, we can replace the damaged key shell without replacing the entire key. This helps restore the key's appearance and functionality while offering a more cost-effective solution.

    My Key Turns, But My Nissan Won't Start. What Could Be Wrong?

    Your Nissan may not start due to a key, transponder, programming, or ignition issue. We can inspect the system and find the cause.

    How Long Does It Take to Replace and Program a Nissan or Infiniti Key?

    The time required depends on your vehicle's model, year, and the type of key you need. In most cases, key cutting and programming can be completed during a single visit, allowing you to get back on the road as quickly as possible.

    Can You Replace a Lost Nissan or Infiniti Key Without the Original?

    Yes. Our locksmiths can cut and program a new key even if you've lost your only key. Using your vehicle's information and specialized programming equipment, we can create a replacement key that's compatible with your Nissan or Infiniti.

    What Should I Do If My Nissan Intelligent Key Isn't Working?

    If your Nissan Intelligent Key isn't working, the problem could be a depleted battery, a programming issue, or a damaged key fob. Our locksmiths can diagnose the cause and suggest the required solution.
