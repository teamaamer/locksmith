    Frequently Asked Questions

    Can You Make A Spare Key Before I Lose My Only One?

    Yes. We can cut and program a spare key while you still have a working one. Having a backup key can save you time and reduce replacement costs. We save you the inconvenience of being locked out or stranded if your primary key is lost or damaged.

    Can You Replace a Damaged Ford or Lincoln Key Shell?

    Yes. A cracked or worn key shell doesn't always mean you need a completely new key. If the internal components are still in good condition, we can transfer them into a new shell, restoring the key's durability and making it more comfortable to use.

    What are the Signs That My Car Lock Needs Service?

    If your key is difficult to insert or turn, the lock feels stiff, the key gets stuck, or the door fails to lock or unlock consistently, it may be time to have your car lock inspected. Addressing these issues early can help prevent complete lock failure and reduce the risk of being locked out of your Ford or Lincoln.

    Can You Remove a Lost or Stolen Key from My Ford or Lincoln?

    Yes. If your Ford or Lincoln key has been lost or stolen, we can reprogram your vehicle's security system to remove the missing key from its memory. This helps prevent the lost key from being used to access or start your vehicle and improves its overall security.

    Do You Provide Locksmith Services for Ford Commercial Vehicles?

    Yes. We provide locksmith services for many Ford commercial vehicles, including Transit vans and fleet vehicles. No matter the lock and key issues, our locksmiths can help minimize downtime and get your vehicle back in service.
