    Frequently Asked Questions

    Do You Work with Mazda Advanced Keyless Entry Systems?

    Yes. Our locksmiths can program compatible Mazda Advanced Keyless Entry smart keys and remotes, ensuring they work correctly with the security system of your car.

    Can You Repair a Key That Has Stopped Unlocking the Doors Remotely?

    Yes. If your Mazda or Mitsubishi remote key is no longer unlocking the doors, the issue may be related to the battery, internal components, or key programming. Our locksmiths can inspect the key, identify the cause, and determine whether it can be repaired or needs to be replaced.

    Can You Replace a Lost Mazda or Mitsubishi Key Without the Original?

    Yes. Losing your only key doesn't always mean you need to visit a dealership. Our locksmiths can create a replacement key for many Mazda and Mitsubishi models by using your vehicle's details and specialized programming equipment.

    Why Isn't My Mazda or Mitsubishi Key Fob Responding?

    A non-responsive key fob can happen for several reasons, including a weak battery, damaged internal components, or signal issues. Our locksmiths can inspect your key fob and vehicle system to identify the cause and recommend the right solution.

    Will a Replacement Key Work Like the Original?

    Yes. When properly cut and programmed, a replacement key is designed to function just like your original key. It can provide the same access and starting capabilities. Our locksmiths ensure the replacement is correctly matched with your vehicle's security system.
